THESS

Piece rules
  • 🧍 Pitbul: steps 1 diagonally forward onto an empty square; captures only the piece directly behind it. Reaching the far rank makes it a Holebul. Holebuls (red or blue bandana) move the same way.
  • 🔺 King (pyramid tank): 1 step any direction, no castling. In check, it may swap with any of your Holebuls if that escapes check. Your last piece becomes a Super King (gold), which moves like a queen, ignores check and can be taken.
  • 🧔 William: always exactly 2 squares in a straight line, capturing enemies on both squares. Blocked by your own piece on either. Passes over holes but dies if it lands on one.
  • 👶 Beeple: captures like a bishop, then steps 1 back along its path. Otherwise 1 step diagonally. A dead Beeple leaves a permanent hole; any piece landing on a hole is taken.
  • 🦅 Hawkathon: no moves of its own. Pick an adjacent enemy (not the king) and fly both pieces to empty squares, keeping the same relative position.
  • 🟫 Cube Queen: slides like a rook, may push up to two enemy pieces in her path sideways, then stops anywhere or captures. Click an enemy to take it, click an empty square to stop there, or drag an enemy piece in her path sideways to push it and move into its square. Any earlier piece she has to push gets a ring: drag it to a dotted square (click elsewhere to cancel). Only a Super King can be pushed.
